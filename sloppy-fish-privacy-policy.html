Privacy Policy for Sloppy Fish

Last updated: December 2, 2025

Developer: Yotam Frid / manfish games

1. Introduction

Sloppy Fish is a simple mobile game developed by Yotam Frid / manfish games.

This privacy policy explains what data the game collects, why it is collected, and how it is used.

I am committed to collecting as little data as possible and keeping all information secure and transparent.

2. Information We Collect

Sloppy Fish does not collect any personal data directly.

However, some third-party services used by the game may collect limited information necessary for their functionality. These services and what they collect are described below.

3. Third-Party Services

3.1 Google AdMob (Non-Personalized Ads Only)

The game displays non-personalized ads, which means:

  • No tracking
  • No ad personalization
  • No cross-app identifiers are used to profile you

AdMob may still collect basic device information required for delivering ads safely and preventing fraud (e.g., country, app version, device type).

Learn more: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3.2 Apple Game Center

If you choose to use Game Center features (such as leaderboards or achievements), Apple may collect information required for your Game Center account. This data is managed by Apple and not by me.

Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

3.3 Google Play Services (Android Only)

On Android, the game uses Google Play Services for achievements, leaderboards, and app functionality. Google may collect basic device and account information required to provide these features.

Google Play privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. In-App Purchases

Sloppy Fish includes one in-app purchase, processed securely through:

  • Apple App Store (iOS)
  • Google Play Store (Android)

The game does not store payment information. All transactions are handled by Apple or Google.

5. Children's Privacy

There is no age restriction on the game.

Because ads are non-personalized and no tracking is used, the game is safe for a general audience.

If you are a parent and believe your child provided personal information directly to me, please contact me and I will remove it. (Note: the game never asks for personal info.)

6. Data Retention

Sloppy Fish does not store any personal data.

Third-party services may retain data according to their own policies.

7. Your Rights

Because Sloppy Fish does not collect personal information directly, there is no data stored that you need to request access to, modify, or delete.

For data handled by Apple, Google, or AdMob, refer to their privacy tools and settings.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
